Disputes & Complaints

Last updated: 5 October 2026

How to raise a problem and how GrowMate handles it.

Where to start

ProblemWhere
Item damaged, wrong, missing or not as describedOpen a refund request from the order in the app within 7 days of delivery
A listing or a user breaking the rulesUse Report on the listing or profile in the app
Seller payout missing or wrongRaise a payout dispute on the payout in Seller Center
Delivery, rider or cash problemEmail support@growmate.online
Copyright or trademarkSee the Intellectual Property Policy
PrivacySee the Privacy Notice
Anything elseEmail support@growmate.online

What to include

  • Your account email and the order reference (shown in the order).
  • What happened and when.
  • Photos or screenshots, for example of the item, the packaging or the chat.
  • What outcome you are asking for.

How GrowMate handles it

  1. We acknowledge your complaint and may ask for more information.
  2. We hear from the other party (for example the seller can respond to a refund request in the app).
  3. We review order, delivery and cash records, including proof-of-delivery photos.
  4. We decide and tell both parties the outcome and the reason.

Target response and resolution times: [TO BE COMPLETED BY GROWMATE].

If you are not satisfied

Reply to our decision to ask for a second review by support@growmate.online. You may also bring consumer complaints to the Department of Trade and Industry and privacy complaints to the National Privacy Commission.
