The problem we're solving

Lots of Filipinos want to grow plants and food at home, and lots of small growers have plants to sell. Four things get in the way.

  1. Advice that ignores our weather

    Much plant-care advice is written for four-season climates. Here, growers deal with wet and dry seasons, typhoons and strong heat, and plants pay the price.

  2. Know-how is scattered

    What to plant, when to plant it and how to keep it alive is spread across posts, chats and word of mouth. It's hard to know what to trust.

  3. Small growers are hard to find

    Backyard growers and small nurseries often depend on walk-ins and word of mouth to reach buyers, even ones who live a few streets away.

  4. Buying from strangers is a gamble

    Paying ahead for a plant from someone you've never met is risky, so many people never try.

One app for the whole growing life

GrowMate puts learning, growing, sharing and trading in one place, so each step leads to the next.

  1. Learn

    The Plant Library has 148 plants you can grow in the Philippines, with local names like pechay, sitaw and malunggay, and the months they’re good to plant.

  2. Grow

    My Garden keeps every plant you own in one place: watering, weather risks, notes and how each one is growing.

  3. Share

    Make your garden public and other growers can visit it in Discover.

  4. Trade

    Buy and sell live plants, seeds and garden supplies with growers near you, paid in cash on delivery.

Keeping the plant economy local

GrowMate is built for buying and selling close to home, so the earning stays with local people.

  • Growers become sellers

    Home growers and small nurseries can sell live plants, seeds and garden supplies from their phone, through the Seller Center.

  • Closer costs less

    Delivery is priced by distance, so buying from a grower near you is the cheaper choice.

  • Local people do the earning

    The seller earns from the sale, and a GrowMate rider earns the delivery fee.

  • Trust is built in

    Verified sellers carry a badge, you pay only when the plant arrives, and every delivery ends with a photo.

Who it helps

GrowMate is built so that everyone around a plant gains something from it.

  • Home growers

    Grow plants and food with confidence, even if it's your first plant, with advice made for the weather where you live.

  • Local sellers & nurseries

    A shop on the phones of buyers nearby. No cash to chase from riders: GrowMate pays you after delivery.

  • Communities & environment

    More food and greenery grown close to home, plants that travel shorter distances, and gardens that pass know-how from neighbour to neighbour.

For sellers

List it, hand it over, get paid. You never have to collect cash from a rider.

  1. List your plants

    Add photos and a price from the Seller Center in the app.

  2. Hand it to the rider

    When an order comes in, a GrowMate rider comes to pick it up. Give them your pickup PIN.

  3. Get paid by GrowMate

    GrowMate pays you after the plant is delivered. The delivery fee is paid by the buyer and goes to the rider.

How buying works

Every plant you buy travels the same route, from the seller to your door, and you pay only when it arrives.

  1. Seller

    Packs your plant and hands it to the rider with a pickup PIN. Only the seller can see the PIN.

  2. Rider

    A GrowMate rider picks the plant up and brings it straight to your address.

  3. Buyer

    You pay the rider in cash: the plant price plus the delivery fee. The rider takes a photo as proof of delivery.

  4. Seller paid

    GrowMate pays the seller after delivery. If something is wrong with your plant, you can ask for a refund in the app.
