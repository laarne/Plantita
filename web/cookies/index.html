Cookie Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This website does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies.

On this website

  • The website (plantita.online) sets no cookies of its own.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google's servers to download them, which shares your IP address and browser details with Google.
  • The site is hosted on Vercel, which processes standard request logs such as IP address to deliver and protect the site.

In the GrowMate web app

  • Sign-in: your session token is kept in the browser's local storage so you stay signed in. It is essential and is removed when you sign out.
  • Preferences and caching: the app stores settings and cached data (for example the marketplace list and weather) on your device so it loads faster and works offline.

GrowMate does not use this storage for advertising or cross-site tracking.

Your choices

You can clear cookies and site data in your browser settings. Clearing GrowMate's storage signs you out and removes cached data.

Changes

If GrowMate adds analytics or other non-essential technologies, this policy will be updated first and, where required, your consent will be asked.
